# Candidate Problems

## Problem 1: Procrastination and Difficulty Staying Consistent

**Who experiences this problem?**

Self-learners who are trying to develop marketable skills, such as learning React or preparing for an AWS certification.

**How do they deal with it today?**

They may use to-do lists, reminders, study schedules, focus timers, or phone-blocking applications. However, these tools can be ignored or turned off when motivation decreases.

**Why does this matter?**

When learners repeatedly postpone their tasks, they may lose consistency and fail to complete their learning goals.

## Problem 2: Difficulty Tracking Progress and Predicting Delays

**Who experiences this problem?**

Self-learners who have a specific learning goal and a deadline, such as an exam date or a certification target.

**How do they deal with it today?**

They may track their progress manually, rely on memory, or estimate how much work remains. This can make it difficult to recognize delays early and determine whether they are still on track to meet their deadline.

**Why does this matter?**

Without a clear comparison between planned and actual progress, learners may discover that they are behind schedule too late to adjust their plans.

## Problem 3: Lack of Accountability

**Who experiences this problem?**

Self-learners who study independently and do not have someone consistently checking their progress.

**How do they deal with it today?**

They may rely on personal motivation, ask friends to support them, or use reminders. However, friends may be too busy to follow up regularly, and reminders can be ignored.

**Why does this matter?**

When learners have no external accountability, it can be easier to skip tasks repeatedly without addressing the problem.

## Relationship Between the Candidates

These three problems are related but distinct. Procrastination concerns completing planned work, progress tracking concerns understanding whether a learner is on schedule, and accountability concerns having an additional reason to stay consistent.

The selected project may address these problems together, while keeping the first version small enough to build and demonstrate within eight weeks.
