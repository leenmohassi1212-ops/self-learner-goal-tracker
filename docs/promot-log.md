# Prompt Log

## V1 — Discover the Real Problem

**Prompt:**

I want to choose a project to build over eight weeks. Do not suggest project ideas yet. Ask me one question at a time to understand a problem I have personally experienced or observed, who faces it, how people currently deal with it, and what solving it would mean. If something is unclear, ask me instead of making assumptions.

**What changed and why:**

I wanted to understand the real problem before choosing a project or jumping directly to a solution.

## V2 — Understand Why Current Solutions Fail

**Prompt:**

Help me investigate why existing solutions do not solve this problem. Consider the limitations of reminders, to-do lists, focus timers, and phone-blocking applications. Ask me questions about what happens when people stop following their plans, and do not assume the reason without asking me.

**What changed and why:**

I shifted the focus from describing the problem to understanding why existing approaches fail and what is missing from them.

## V3 — Explore Accountability as Part of the Solution

**Prompt:**

Help me explore how accountability could address the problem of self-learners abandoning their learning goals. Ask me how a trusted person could be involved, what should trigger a notification, and what consent or practical limitations need to be considered. Ask one question at a time.

**What changed and why:**

I explored accountability as a possible response to the lack of consequences when learners repeatedly miss their tasks.

## V4 — Check Whether the Project Is Realistic

**Prompt:**

Help me define a realistic first version of my self-learning web app. I have eight weeks, will work alone, can spend approximately eight hours per week, and must demonstrate a working application live. I can use one AI subscription but cannot pay for additional services. Prioritize essential features, identify what should be excluded, and explain what to cut if the project becomes too large.

**What changed and why:**

I introduced the actual development constraints so the project would remain achievable within the available time and resources.
