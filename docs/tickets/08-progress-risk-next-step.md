# 08 Progress, risk and next step

Why
The visible half of the product. One calculation module feeds the dashboard and the emails so the two can never disagree (R5, R6, R7, N3, `rules.md`).

Done when
R5's two worked examples pass exactly (10 tasks / 20 days / day 10 / 3 done → planned 5, behind 2; 10 tasks / 30 days → planned 3, behind 0), with day 0 start and rounding down. R6's thresholds show On track / At risk / High risk at 0, 1 and 2 behind on 10 tasks, with the word "estimate" beside the level everywhere (N3). The next step is the next unticked task, unless the learner picked one, which sticks until ticked or changed (R7). Adding a task recalculates the plan (R3).

Touches
Calculation module, S4 active state, goal and task reads.
