# 10 Warning and stop emails

Why
This is what the chosen person actually experiences; it must feel like a warning, not a punishment (`research.md`), and it must never say more than it is allowed to (N6, N7, N3).

Done when
The learner's and the chosen person's warning emails exist as templates in one module (`rules.md`). The chosen person's contains only: the learner's name, the goal's name and deadline, time since the last completed task, the risk level marked "estimate", and — once true — that the deadline passed (N6); no task names. Every email to them carries a stop link (N7). Clicking stop ends all emails to them for that goal, shows "stopped" on the learner's dashboard, and unlocks replacement (R8). The email states the real time since the last task (A24).

Touches
Email module, stop route, S5 stopped view, S4 person-stopped state.
