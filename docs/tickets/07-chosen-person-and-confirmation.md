# 07 Chosen person and confirmation

Why
The consequence is the product, and it cannot exist until someone the learner chose has agreed to it; the goal cannot start without them (R8, N1, A17).

Done when
R8's whole done-when passes: the learner's own address is refused; the request states the deadline; no request goes out while the goal has no tasks; status stays pending until the confirm link is clicked; ticking is refused until then; a non-confirmer can be replaced, the replacement starting at pending; after the start, replacement is refused unless they stopped. Confirming on S5 starts the goal at that moment — plan and 48-hour clock both (R2, A17). A confirm link on an ended goal is refused (R13). A pending person receives nothing but the confirmation email (N1).

Touches
S2 chosen-person card, S5 page, S4 waiting state, chosen-person table, goal start, email module.
