# Screens

Five screens.

S1 Sign up / log in: a learner gets into their account. Asks for their name; their timezone is read from the browser. Also where a learner asks for a password reset and sets a new password from the link. Serves R1, N8.

S2 Goal: set the target, type, deadline, career note, and chosen person's name and email. Also where the learner replaces a chosen person and, until the goal starts, edits or abandons it. Serves R2, R8, R13, N11.

S3 Tasks: add, edit and reorder the goal's tasks; delete only before the goal starts. Serves R3.

S4 Dashboard: shows a waiting message until the chosen person confirms. After that: tick tasks, see planned vs actual, delay, risk estimate, next step, chosen person's status, and the AI suggestion if it survives. Once the goal has completed or ended: the outcome, the final count, and a way to start a new goal. Serves R4, R5, R6, R7, R8, R10, R12, R13, N3.

S5 Chosen person page: opened from an email link with no login. They confirm, or stop future emails. Serves R8, N1, N7.

Emails are not screens, but five exist: the confirmation request, the no-task warning to the learner, the no-task warning to the chosen person, the missed-deadline email to both, and the password reset. Serve R1, R8, R9, R13, N6.

Not building: analytics or history pages, a settings area, an admin area, a page for the chosen person to view progress, a native mobile app, or browser push notifications.
