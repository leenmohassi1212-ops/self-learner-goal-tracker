# Screens

Five screens. `[ASSUMED A14: the count and the split are the model's draft]`

S1 Sign up / log in: a learner gets into their account. Asks for their name; their timezone is read from the browser. `[ASSUMED A19]` Serves R1, N8.

S2 Goal: set the target, type, deadline, career note, and chosen person's name and email. Also where the learner replaces a chosen person. Serves R2, R8, N11.

S3 Tasks: add, edit and reorder the goal's tasks; delete only before the goal starts. Serves R3.

S4 Dashboard: shows a waiting message until the chosen person confirms. After that: tick tasks, see planned vs actual, delay, risk estimate, next step, chosen person's status, and the AI suggestion if it survives. Serves R4, R5, R6, R7, R8, R10, R12, R13, N3.

S5 Chosen person page: opened from an email link with no login. They confirm, or stop future emails. Serves R8, N1, N7.

Emails are not screens, but three exist: the confirmation request, the no-task warning to the learner, and the no-task warning to the chosen person. Serve R8, R9, N6.

Not building: analytics or history pages, a settings area, an admin area, a page for the chosen person to view progress, a native mobile app, or browser push notifications.
