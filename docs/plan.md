# Self-Learner Goal Tracker - plan

A web app for self-learners who keep postponing a market skill such as an AWS certification or React. The learner sets a goal with a deadline and breaks it into daily tasks. The app shows how far behind plan they are and the estimated risk of missing the deadline. After 48 hours with no task completed, it emails both the learner and a person they chose, who must confirm first. There is no code yet. Built alone over eight weeks with no paid services.
